# People and Ideas

This area traces how technical ideas developed through the problems people were trying to solve. It is not a collection of conventional biographies.

Useful entries explain:

- the problem and historical setting;
- what was known before the contribution;
- the new assumption, model, method, or evidence;
- disagreements and limitations;
- how later work revised the idea;
- where the idea appears in modern equations or software.

Prefer primary sources and careful historical scholarship. Clearly separate documented history from interpretation, and link each entry to the questions it helps answer.
